Chris Leung

Third-year Natural Sciences student at the University of Cambridge, based in the Department of Genetics. My interests lie at the intersection of machine learning and genetics — how we navigate data domains which are spatiotemporally sparse, heterogeneous, and unreliable.

I am currently a Research Fellow of the Supervised Program for Alignment Research, with a particular interest in the limitations of behavioural evaluations.

Perhaps the most fascinating/consequential story of the last 15 years, but the significance of what Demis and his team have achieved is eclipsed by the book's focus on the more procedural aspects of building DeepMind. I wish he had more space to expand on how the scientific method became a winning formula for an AGI lab, years before anyone else dared to dream of the possibilities.

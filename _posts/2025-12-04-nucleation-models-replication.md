Notes:

DNA replication results in forked DNA: this process fires from multiple locations. The timing of replication refers to when a locus on the genome 'fires', or is reached by a fork. What patterns can we find in this process?

Other concurrent processes (transcription, protein interactions) complicate the dynamics of replication. Some might take a neural net-based approach, but Berkemeier et al. (2025) derive a close-formed relationship between timing and firing.

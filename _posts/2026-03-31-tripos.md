The Cambridge Tripos (examination) system is an anachronistic peculiarity. For the most part, none of the work you do during the year counts at all. And it all comes down to a couple of three-hour exams in stuffy old rooms.

I'd love to say that I've gotten the hang of the examination system, but then I'd be kidding myself. However, there are some enforceable goals that I'm aiming to apply this year.

1. Focus on the format: unfortunately, the examination system is very gameable, and falls foul of Goodhart's law. So leverage this to your advantage!
2. Here comes the sun: you only have one more year of being an undergraduate at Cambridge! Take advantage of the great outdoors as a motivator. Get your work done, and get outside - enjoy the sunshine! (Or rain, snow, and hail)
3. Your friends are smart: exams are rarely a zero-sum game amongst small groups of people, and I'd argue that zero-sum thinking in general is detrimental to your performance. Work together to patch your weaknesses!

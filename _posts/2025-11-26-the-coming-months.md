I am digging deep for the next few months, to focus on machine learning and mathematics. My motivation stems from the following equation for universal intelligence proposed by Legg and Hutter (2007):

$$\Upsilon(\pi)=\sum_{\mu\in E}2^{-K(\mu)}V^{\pi}_{\mu}$$

This provides a description of *general* intelligence: such systems can accomplish tasks in a variety of environments, with a penalty term that scales with the task's Kolmogorov complexity.

How will I accomplish this? The resources on the Internet are noisy, but I think I've come up with a solid curriculum to iterate on. Along the way, I plan to write about concepts I find interesting, particularly in cooperative AI and reinforcement learning. The field represents a balance between theory (mathematics/concepts) and practice (the art of programming), and I will try to balance the two.

Stay tuned for more!

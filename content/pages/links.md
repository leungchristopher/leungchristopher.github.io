Schools and universities have a knack of convincing people that hard technical problems exist in nicely sandboxed environments: [Ben Kuhn](https://www.benkuhn.net/hard/) provides my favourite counter-justification.

[A future history of biomedical progress](https://markovbio.github.io/biomedical-progress/): an interesting wishlist. [Impetus Grants](https://norn.group/impetus-grants) does really cool grant work for biotech. Thinking about [tacit knowledge](https://commoncog.com/the-tacit-knowledge-series/) is also quite important.

I return to [Siddhartha](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2500) and [The Glass Bead Game](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/355262/the-glass-bead-game-by-hesse-hermann/9780099283621) every now and then.

A few books I would wholeheartedly recommend:
- The making of the atomic bomb (Richard Rhodes)
- The art of gathering: how we meet and why it matters (Priya Parker)
- Digital minimalism (Cal Newport)
- The concept of law (H.L.A. Hart)
- Impro: improvisation and the theatre (Keith Johnstone)
- Conjectures and refutations: the growth of scientific knowledge (Karl Popper)
- The technological singularity (Murray Shanahan)

Some aesthetically pleasing music: [Verklärte Nacht (Arnold Schoenberg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verkl%C3%A4rte_Nacht), [Brahms' 3rd piano quartet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piano_Quartet_No._3_(Brahms)), and [Rachmaninoff's Suite no.2 for two pianos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suite_No._2_(Rachmaninoff)). Also, [Brian Eno's Music for Airports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambient_1:_Music_for_Airports), which ironically was too interesting for airports.

- [Genome-wide RNAi against Covid](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-023-04589-5): an interesting application of massively parallel siRNA discovery.
- [Anti-aging strategies and ex vivo organ rejuvenation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S193459092500445X)
- [Transposons and aging](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/scitranslmed.abl6057/)
- [The data center as a computer](https://andreybleme.com/2026-09-22/luiz-andre-barroso/)

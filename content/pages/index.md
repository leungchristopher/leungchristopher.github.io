I work at the intersection of neuroscience, genetics, and machine learning: intelligent systems that learn to adapt and cooperate.
My research is currently split between the [University of Cambridge](https://cam.ac.uk) (mathematical genetics), [Caltech](https://www.caltech.edu/) (neuroscience), and a new venture.

### What motivates me

I am a general, technical problem solver (see below). I am currently interested in robotics, reasoning, and large-scale deployment of genetics in the biotech/biomedical field. If you're interested in these ideas, I would love to [hear from you](https://calendly.com/chcl4-cam/30min).

### About me

I studied Natural Sciences at the [University of Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/) from 2023 to 2026, obtaining a double First and the Holgate-Pollard Prize and J.M. Thoday Prize.

In a past life, I was a finalist in the selection of the UK's teams for the International Chemistry Olympiad and [International Astronomy and Astrophysics Olympiad](https://www.bpho.org.uk/IOAA/). I also received distinctions/top golds in the British [Mathematical](https://bmos.ukmt.org.uk/) and [Physics](https://www.bpho.org.uk) Olympiads, and the Freeman Dyson Prize in Physics. My team and I also won the [National Cipher Challenge](https://www.cipherchallenge.org/) in 2022.

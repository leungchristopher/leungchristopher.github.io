I am Chris Leung, and I find neuroscience, genetics, and machine learning fascinating. I am interested in the development of intelligent systems that learn to adapt and cooperate. My research is currently split between the [University of Cambridge](https://cam.ac.uk) (mathematical and computational genetics), [Caltech](https://www.caltech.edu/) (neuroscience), and a new venture.

I studied Natural Sciences at the [University of Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/) from 2023 to 2026, obtaining a double First, where I placed second in my cohort and received the Holgate-Pollard and J.M. Thoday prize.

In a past life, I was a finalist in the selection of the UK's teams for the International Chemistry Olympiad and [International Astronomy and Astrophysics Olympiad](https://www.bpho.org.uk/IOAA/). I also received distinctions/top golds in the British [Mathematical](https://bmos.ukmt.org.uk/) and [Physics](https://www.bpho.org.uk) Olympiads, and the Freeman Dyson Prize in Physics. My team and I also won the [National Cipher Challenge](https://www.cipherchallenge.org/) in 2022.

Beyond my work, I enjoy playing chamber music, tennis, and distance running.

If you are interested in collaborating with me, feel free to reach out by [email](mailto:chrishcleung@gmail.com).

[The illustrated AlphaFold](https://elanapearl.github.io/blog/2024/the-illustrated-alphafold/)

The input starts as a series of sequences and molecules. AlphaFold 3's features and tokens are complicated by the need to represent small molecules, DNA/RNA, and proteins, all as sequences. Following tokenisation, single/pair representations on the atom and token level are made, alongside an MSA and template. Atom-wise tokens for non-standard amino acids, nucleotides, and molecules.

Conquering rollercoasters seems to be a remarkably transferable and practical skill. Looking up at Goliath at Six Flags Magic Mountain, one can't help but feel awed, perhaps dazed by the 250-foot drop that beckons. Yet you strap yourself into the harness, think pleasant thoughts as you move up the lift ramp, and try to keep your head straight whilst others are losing theirs on the way down. And when you complete the cycle, you leave the ride exhilirated, ready to search for your next target.

It's a fantastic flow state.

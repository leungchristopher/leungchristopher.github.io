On the unknown

11 September 2025

I've been thinking about the great unknowns in a career of discovery and research. We may have intuitions about where our work might lead us, but the accuracy of our predictions is incommensurable. Perhaps the most useful thing we can do is to develop better priors.

New beginnings are often perilous and exhilirating, and I look forward to finding out what deep learning and AI safety lead me to.
